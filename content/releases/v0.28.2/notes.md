## Improvements

- Joining or reopening a game now loads more reliably for guests, with less chance of a stalled lobby or missing room controls.
- Private poem downloads and social previews work again, so sharing a finished poem is smoother.
- Behind the scenes, Linejam's data now lives closer to the game itself, which keeps saves and restores quicker and more dependable.
- Error tracking now separates distinct problems, helping the team spot and fix issues faster.
