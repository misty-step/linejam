## Transfer principles

These are design inferences to evaluate, not measured outcomes:

- Make arrival, acknowledgement, and reveal memorable; let writing and reading remain calm.
- Withhold poem content, not understanding of the current phase or next action.
- Celebrate successful delivery, never score or judge the writing.
- Carry a stable avatar, pen name, and readable status rather than relying on color.
- Let people, not autoplay or an announcer, own the reveal.
- Original illustration, distinct typography, and one meaningful motion can carry joy without decoration density.
- Silence, reduced motion, zoom, dictation, and keyboard operation must remain first-class.

The five independently authored directions in `explorations/renewal/brief.json` use these principles, not reference assets. Static artwork does not establish timing or sound behavior. Source pages document maker claims; they are not independent usability studies. No copyrighted reference logos, characters, screenshots, or audio have been added to product assets.
