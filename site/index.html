A little room for words.

A poetry game for people who don't have to be poets.

Write a line. Pass it on.

Start a game Join a room

Play on your own phones, together in the same room. No account needed.

One line leads to another.

  1. Get together

    One person creates a room. Everyone else joins with the code and a pen name.

  2. Write nine lines

    Each round, you see only the line before yours. The word count grows from one to five, then back to one.

  3. Read them aloud

    Each person gets a complete poem to read. Find out what you made together.

Your poems stay yours.

Poems are private by default. You choose when to make a public link, and you can revoke it later.

Play as a guest, or create an account to access your poems on other devices. Every new line comes from someone in your room.
